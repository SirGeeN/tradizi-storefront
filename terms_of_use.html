6. Cloud Backup and Data Sync

Important — Free Account Users: Free plan accounts receive 90 days of cloud backup from registration. Please read this section carefully.

6.1 Free Plan Backup Period

Free plan accounts are entitled to 90 days of cloud backup and sync from registration. During this period, your data is automatically backed up to our secure cloud servers.

6.2 End of Free Backup Period

  • Days 76–85: Gentle reminder that your cloud backup period is ending soon
  • Days 86–89: Urgent reminder that cloud backup stops in a few days
  • Day 90 onwards: New data will no longer be backed up to the cloud. The app continues to function on your device using local data only. Locally stored data is not deleted.

6.3 Data Access After Backup Stops

After the free backup period, you may still access data backed up during the first 90 days. New records created after day 90 will not be stored in the cloud unless you upgrade.
