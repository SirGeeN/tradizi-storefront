Our Mission

Replacing Notebooks With Smart Business Infrastructure

Millions of businesses in Nigeria run their operations on notebooks, memory, and scattered WhatsApp messages. Important data gets lost. Debts go untracked. Stock runs out unexpectedly. Profits are unknown.

TRADIZI was built to change that. We believe every business — no matter how big or small — deserves a reliable, intelligent system to manage their daily operations with confidence.

Our goal is simple: make business management so easy that any owner can master it in minutes, and so powerful that it scales with them as they grow.

Our Story

Why We Built TRADIZI

TRADIZI started with a simple observation: business owners in Nigeria are among the hardest working people in the world — yet they were managing their businesses with tools that hadn't changed in decades.

A trader selling rice and beans in Lagos kept her stock in a notebook. A phone accessories shop owner in Abuja tracked debts in his head. A cosmetics business owner relied on WhatsApp voice messages to remember daily sales. When things went wrong — and they always did — there was no system to fall back on.

We built TRADIZI to be the operating system for Nigerian commerce. Offline-first because electricity and internet are unreliable. Simple because business owners don't have time for complex software. Powerful because growing businesses need real intelligence — not just a digital notebook.

Every feature in TRADIZI was shaped by real conversations with real business owners. The bulk product tracking, the tap-to-sell grid, the WhatsApp receipt sharing, the daily summary notification — all of it came from watching how businesses actually work, and building exactly what they need.
