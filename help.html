Register your business

Two ways to create your account — pick whichever is easiest for you.

Option A — Continue with Google (fastest)
  1. On the welcome screen, tap Continue with Google.
  2. Choose the Google account you want to use for your business.
  3. Enter your business name when prompted, and you're in — no password to remember.
Option B — Phone number & PIN
  1. On the welcome screen, tap Register with Phone.
  2. Enter your business name and phone number.
  3. Create a 4-digit PIN — you'll use this to log in from now on. Choose one you won't forget; write it down somewhere safe.
  4. Confirm your PIN and you're ready to go.
Tip: If you ever move to a new phone, use the same Google account or phone number to log back in — all your data (products, sales, customers) will restore automatically.
2

Add your products

Inventory tab → tap the + button
  1. Enter the product name and choose a category.
  2. Enter your cost price (what you paid) and selling price (what customers pay).
  3. Enter your current stock quantity.
  4. If you sell in packages (e.g. a bag, carton, or crate of items), turn on Packaging and enter how many units are in one package, plus the package price.
  5. If the same product comes in different options (e.g. sizes or colors), turn on Variants instead of packaging — add each size/color combination with its own stock and price.
  6. Add a photo if you have one — this shows on your online store.
  7. Tap Save. Your product now appears on the Sales screen, ready to sell.
Important: Always enter a real selling price above ₦0. A product saved with no price will ask you to confirm the price again the first time you try to sell it.
3

Record a sale

Sales tab
  1. Tap any product to add it straight to your cart — one tap per item sold.
  2. Need more of the same item? Tap it again, or use the +/− buttons in the cart to adjust quantity.
  3. Selling a product you haven't added to Inventory yet? Tap Sell New Item at the top — this lets you record the sale immediately and adds it to your Inventory automatically.
  4. When you're done, tap Checkout, choose how the customer paid (Cash, Transfer, Card, or Credit), and confirm.
  5. A receipt is generated automatically — you can share it on WhatsApp or as a PDF straight after.
Selling on credit? Choose Credit as the payment method and enter the customer's details — this automatically logs it as debt owed, trackable from the Credit tab (see section 9).
4

Set up your online store

More tab → Online Store
  1. Turn on Make My Store Public — this is off by default until you enable it.
  2. Choose whether to show prices on your storefront, or keep them hidden (customers message you to ask).
  3. Your storefront link is generated automatically: tradizi.com/shop/yourstore — share this on WhatsApp status, Instagram bio, or anywhere else.
  4. Customers can order two ways: message you on WhatsApp directly with their order, or pay online instantly (Pay Now) if you've set up a payout account (see section 5).
  5. Any product you don't want visible online can be hidden per-product from the same screen — useful for items that are out of season or reserved.
Note: Your storefront link is also included automatically on every receipt you share, so past customers can always find their way back to shop again.
5

Set up your payout account

Owner only
More tab → Payout Account

This is what lets customers pay you instantly online through your storefront's Pay Now option — money goes straight to your own bank account.

  1. Select your bank from the list.
  2. Enter your account number.
  3. TRADIZI verifies the account and shows you the account holder's name — double-check this matches you before continuing. This step exists specifically so you never accidentally link the wrong account.
  4. Confirm, and you're done — Pay Now is now active on your storefront.
Without this step: customers can still order via WhatsApp, but the Pay Now instant-payment option won't be available until your payout account is set up.
6

Set up delivery zones

Owner only
More tab → Delivery Zones
  1. Tap Add Zone and name it (e.g. "Mainland", "Island", "Within 5km").
  2. Enter the delivery fee for that zone — or leave it as Free if you don't charge for that area.
  3. Add as many zones as you need. Customers select their zone at checkout, and the correct fee is added automatically.
  4. If a customer's area isn't listed, your storefront shows a note asking them to message you directly to arrange delivery.
Tip: Until you add a zone, delivery is free by default for everyone — set this up early if you charge for delivery anywhere.
7

Add staff & set permissions

Owner only
More tab → Staff & Permissions
  1. Tap Add Staff and enter their name and phone number.
  2. Choose their role: Manager (broad access, can see cost/profit if you allow it) or Seller (records sales only, no access to costs, editing, or deleting).
  3. An invite code is generated — share this with your staff member.
  4. They enter the code in their own TRADIZI app to join your business under their own login.
  5. You can edit a staff member's permissions or remove their access at any time from the same screen.
Why this matters: Sellers can record sales and view their own performance, but can never see your cost prices, profit margins, or delete/edit records — your numbers stay protected.
8

Record expenses

More tab → Expenses
  1. Tap the + button.
  2. Choose a category (Rent, Utilities, Transport, Staff Costs, Supplies, and more).
  3. Enter the amount and a short description.
  4. Save — this expense is now automatically deducted when calculating your Profit After Expenses on the Dashboard and Reports.
Two profit numbers, on purpose: Your Dashboard shows both Gross Profit (sales minus cost of goods) and Profit After Expenses (Gross Profit minus everything you've logged here) — side by side, so you always see both the full picture and the real bottom line.
9

Track customer credit (debt)

Credit tab
  1. Every sale recorded as Credit (see section 3) automatically appears here, grouped by customer.
  2. Tap any customer to see their full balance and payment history.
  3. When they pay you back — in full or in part — tap Collect Payment and enter the amount. Their balance updates immediately.
  4. Turn on debt reminder notifications (see section 11) to get nudged automatically about outstanding balances.
10

Reports

Reports tab
  1. Sales — revenue, gross and net profit, and your top-selling products, filterable by day, week, month, or a custom date range.
  2. Inventory — stock levels, low-stock alerts, and inventory value at a glance.
  3. Staff (Owner only) — see revenue and expenses broken down per staff member, so you know exactly who sold what.
  4. Every report can be exported as a PDF to share or keep for your own records.
11

Notifications

More tab → Notifications
  1. Daily Report — get a summary of the day's sales and profit pushed to your phone at a time you choose.
  2. Debt Reminders — get reminded about customers who owe you money, so nothing slips through the cracks.
  3. Turn either on or off, and set your preferred time for each, from this same screen.
12

Upgrading your plan

TRADIZI is free to start. Pro and Business unlock unlimited products, advanced reports, multi-device access, and more.

  1. From More tab → your plan name at the bottom of the menu shows your current plan.
  2. To upgrade, visit tradizi.com/subscribe.html from any browser.
  3. Choose Pro or Business, pick a billing period, and enter the email or phone number you registered with.
  4. Complete payment — your account is upgraded automatically within seconds, no need to contact anyone.
Renewing early? Paying again before your current plan expires simply adds the new period on top of your remaining time — you never lose days by renewing early.
